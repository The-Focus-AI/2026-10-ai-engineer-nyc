# Ninety percent of enterprises rolled out an LLM. Five percent run agents outside engineering.

Opening the afternoon keynote block on Day 1, Tenex co-founder Alex Lieberman shared field numbers from roughly a hundred large enterprises across financial services, healthcare, and industrials. Access to chat models is nearly universal, yet production agents outside software engineering have barely started.

## Four milestones of enterprise AI diffusion: 90%, 60%, 50%, 5%

Recounting a backstage conversation with conference co-organizer swyx (transcribed as "Switz"), Lieberman laid out how AI adoption drops across roughly a hundred large enterprises Tenex advises in financial services, healthcare, and industrials (00:02:29). He framed enterprise rollout as a four-milestone race. First, roughly 90% of those organizations have rolled out a general-purpose LLM to employees (00:02:29). Second, about 60% have trained staff on how to apply those interfaces in daily work (00:02:29).

The steeper drop arrives with autonomous workflows. Around 50% have put coding agents into active engineering and product delivery, making software teams the clear beachhead (00:02:29). Outside engineering, production agents working in business workloads sit at roughly 5% (00:02:29). Alongside open-source versus fine-tuned model choices and the difficulty of hiring AI engineers at scale, closing that 90-to-5 gap defines the work of the next 12 to 36 months before Lieberman hands the stage to Coatue's Frank Long (00:02:29).

**Key points**

- Across ~100 large enterprises Tenex advises, ~90% have deployed a general-purpose LLM and ~60% have trained employees to use it.
- Roughly 50% run coding agents inside engineering and product workflows.
- Only ~5% run production agents in non-engineering workloads, leaving a 12-to-36-month enterprise diffusion gap.

> “Next up is coding agents, agents that are actually used within engineering and product workflows — that's around 50% of all the enterprises we've been in, and then the final one where we are truly still day one is agents actually working in non-engineering workloads in an organization, that is around five percent right now.”
> — 00:02:29
