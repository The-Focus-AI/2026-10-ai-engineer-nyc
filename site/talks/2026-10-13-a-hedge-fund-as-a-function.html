A Hedge Fund as a Function

Frank Long, Coatue

A hedge fund can be engineered as a function from external and internal context to a stock/size/timing decision, decomposed into steps whose runners (code, statistics, LLMs, people) and settings are tuned like hyperparameters against an evaluable north star.
