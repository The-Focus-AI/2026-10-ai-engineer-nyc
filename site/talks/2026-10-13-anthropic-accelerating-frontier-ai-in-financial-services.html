Anthropic: Accelerating frontier AI in Financial Services.

Belinda Neal, Anthropic · Keon Shahab, Anthropic

Because frontier models now outpace adoption, financial firms should stop hand-building agents one by one and build a reusable five-layer foundation (MCP connectors, skills, identity and permissions, human approval, evals) that lets them scale agents safely.

Theme 03 · 7 talksThe Diffusion Gap

Model capability has outrun adoption: outside coding, agents barely reach real enterprise work, and the bottleneck is process, trust and setup friction rather than intelligence.

Theme 06 · 5 talksBounded Autonomy

Agents become trustworthy the way organizations already trust non-deterministic people: through declared permissions, written policy, risk-tiered review and opinionated process, not by locking them down or approving every action.

Theme 07 · 5 talksProvenance and Grounding in Financial AI

In finance, an agent earns a role in real decisions only when it runs on the firm's own governed, time-scoped data with a validated golden source, because the data and its connectors matter more than the model.
