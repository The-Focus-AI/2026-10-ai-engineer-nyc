Anthropic: Accelerating frontier AI in Financial Services.

Belinda Neal, Anthropic · Keon Shahab, Anthropic

Because frontier model capability, cost efficiency, and prompt-injection safety now outpace enterprise diffusion, financial institutions must shift from bespoke one-off agent builds to a reusable five-layer architecture combining MCP data connectivity, SME-authored versioned skills, least-privilege identity, human-approved sandboxed execution, and continuous regression evals.
