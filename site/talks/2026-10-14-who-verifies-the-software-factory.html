Who verifies the software factory?

Manish Kapur, Sonar · Addy Osmani, Anthropic

Because agents now open most pull requests and few teams review every line, a software factory can only move toward autonomy if every loop carries independent, layered automated verification (deterministic analysis, agentic review, supply-chain checks) plus agent-generated evidence that the work is correct.
