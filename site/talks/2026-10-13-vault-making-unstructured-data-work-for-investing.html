Vault: Making Unstructured Data Work for Investing

Aaron Linsky, Bridgewater · Sam Green, Bridgewater

Bridgewater argues that AI only becomes useful for investing when it sits on a governed, ontology-aligned corpus of internal and external unstructured data, served to both humans and agents and wired into investors' existing habits so their work compounds.
