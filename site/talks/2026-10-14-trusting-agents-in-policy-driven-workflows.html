Trusting Agents in Policy-Driven Workflows

Evan Kormos, Coinbase

Trusting agents in regulated workflows starts by treating written policy as the institution's original intent, separating it from the constantly changing running system, and placing agents inside gated workflow graphs that keep required checks.
