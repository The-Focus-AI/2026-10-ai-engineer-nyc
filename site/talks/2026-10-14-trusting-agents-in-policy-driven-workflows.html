Trusting Agents in Policy-Driven Workflows

Evan Kormos, Coinbase

Deploying agents safely in regulated enterprise workflows requires treating written policy as normative build-time intent separated from the continuously changing runtime system, and embedding agents inside directed acyclic graphs (DAGs) that parallelize independent reviews while preserving mandatory compliance gates.
