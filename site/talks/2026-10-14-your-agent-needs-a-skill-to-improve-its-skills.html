Your agent needs a skill to improve its skills

Ben Holmes, Warp

Agents can improve their own setup if every conversation is centralized with its tool calls and outputs, graded by LLM-as-judge scorers against team rubrics, and the failures are periodically handed to a self-improvement agent that proposes skill changes whose effect shows up in tracked scores.
