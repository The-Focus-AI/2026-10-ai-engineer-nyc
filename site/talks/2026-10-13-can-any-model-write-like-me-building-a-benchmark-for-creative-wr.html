Can Any Model Write Like Me? Building a Benchmark for Creative Writing

Louis-François Bouchard, Towards AI

AI writing slop does not go away as models scale, so Towards AI built a voice-matching creative-writing benchmark with multiple drafts and cross-family LLM judges, and found the practical goal is the model least annoying to edit, not a perfect one-shot.
