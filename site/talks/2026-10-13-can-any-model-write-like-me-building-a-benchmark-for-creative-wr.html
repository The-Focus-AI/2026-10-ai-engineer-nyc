Can Any Model Write Like Me? Building a Benchmark for Creative Writing

Louis-François Bouchard, Towards AI

Because AI writing slop persists as models scale, Towards AI built ToneBench—evaluating 120 models across ten long-prompt tasks, five drafts per prompt, two-stage categorical rubrics, and cross-family LLM judges—showing that multi-draft cheap generation merged by a reasoning model beats expensive one-shot prose.
