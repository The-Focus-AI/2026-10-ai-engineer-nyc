Balancing Acceleration and Safety for AI Agents

John Feminella, Two Sigma

Two Sigma gives agents real autonomy safely by composing lightweight controls — an agent registry, risk-tiered review, declared tool entitlements enforced at runtime, shared evals, and classifier-gated approvals — instead of locking everything down or relying on policy documents.

Agents become trustworthy the way organizations already trust non-deterministic people: through declared permissions, written policy, risk-tiered review and opinionated process, not by locking them down or approving every action.
