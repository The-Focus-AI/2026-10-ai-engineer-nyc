Agents can pay. So why don’t they?

Jan Curn, Apify

Despite an explosion of crypto and fintech agent payment standards, autonomous agent transaction volume remains negligible because pre-provisioned API keys, subsidized flat-rate token plans, wallet setup friction, untrusted service discovery, and deferred-settlement double-spend risks suppress demand across every stage of the payment funnel.
