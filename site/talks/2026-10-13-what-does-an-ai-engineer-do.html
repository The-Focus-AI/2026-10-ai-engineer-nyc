What does an AI engineer do?

Aparna Dhinakaran, Arize AI

The AI engineer's role has shifted from manually building and reviewing individual features to architecting closed-loop agent factories — where agent-first telemetry triggers isolated, durable sandbox runs, LLM judges are meta-evaluated against human judgment, and both production and coding agents are traced end-to-end.
