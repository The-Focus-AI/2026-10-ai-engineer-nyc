00:02:12 — A Hiring Agent Project for Conference Attendees

Now, a couple of words: how my day went. I spend the day talking to other speakers and participants, and what I've noticed is that one of the strongest incentives to come to this conference is hiring, to the point that even some speakers are hiring and also looking for work.

So, I have a little idea for you about how to benefit the most from this amazing conference, and make sure the financial part gets some thoughtful attention too, so I suggest you build an agent that takes the conference program and makes a list of all the speakers and their companies. Then you rank them yourself based on whose work and talk you found the most interesting, and who you would actually like to work with.

The next step is to send your agent to those companies' careers pages, right, and find them open positions to match them to your experience, and collect the published compensation ranges. Then look at your ranking again, and if the salary changes your mind and changes your ranking, that's fine. You are updating your beliefs based on the evidence. So, now, while you still here, I encourage you to go and find the people you would really like to work with or meet and talk to them, because you've heard their talks and you know something about their work, and that would be just such a good conversation starter.

And please don't make your agents send out your resume automatically. That's basically that carbonell. So, this is the project for you for the rest of the day or the trip home.
