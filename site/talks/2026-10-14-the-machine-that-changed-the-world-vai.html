Theme 05 · 6 talksVerification Loops Over Human Review

Because agents now produce code and work faster than people can read it, trust has to come from independent, deterministic verification loops that prove each outcome with evidence, not from humans reviewing every line.

Contested fault lineCan an LLM judge be trusted to grade agent output?

Here: No: LLM verdicts change across identical replications and when challenged, so they are unreliable verifiers.
