From Model to Metrics: Serving and Benchmarking Any LLM Live with vLLM and Runpod

Chris Vela, Runpod

Rather than deploying open-weight LLMs with default vLLM recipes and oversized context windows, Runpod's BenchPod wraps Dockerized vLLM builds with live YAML reconfiguration, self-healing watchdogs, and FastAPI benchmark endpoints so teams and agents can empirically tune throughput and latency before scaling out.
